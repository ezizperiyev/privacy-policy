Satomi — Mobile Game

Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2025  ·  Version 1.0

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Satomi handles your information — or rather, how we deliberately choose not to collect it. We built this game to be played, not to harvest data.

01

No Data Collection

Satomi does not collect, store, or process any personal information about its players. We do not request your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other identifying information. The game is fully self-contained and functions entirely without an account or registration.

  • No personal information is requested or required to play
  • No account creation or login is needed
  • No usage analytics or behavioral tracking is performed

02

No Data Storage

We do not maintain any databases, servers, or cloud infrastructure that store player data. Any game progress or settings saved by the app are stored locally on your own device and remain entirely under your control. Uninstalling the application removes all locally stored data.

03

No Sharing or Disclosure

Because we collect no data, we have nothing to share. We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose any user information to third parties — not to advertisers, data brokers, analytics companies, or any other organization.

  • No data sold to advertisers or third parties
  • No sharing with analytics platforms or data brokers
  • No disclosure to any government or legal entity beyond lawful obligation

04

No Tracking or Cookies

Satomi does not use cookies, tracking pixels, device fingerprinting, advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), or any other technology designed to monitor your activity inside or outside the game.

05

Third-Party Services

If Satomi is distributed through an app store (such as Google Play or the Apple App Store), that platform may collect limited technical data in accordance with its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review the policies of the respective platform. We have no control over and take no responsibility for data collected by those third parties.

06

Children's Privacy

Satomi does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else. The game is safe for all ages precisely because it collects nothing. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us — though we have no mechanism through which that could occur.

07

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify users through an in-app notice or the app store listing. We will never introduce data collection practices without clearly informing you first.
